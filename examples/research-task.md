# Research Task Example

## Objective

Compare current primary-source guidance on a specified technical question.

## Boundaries

- Prefer official documentation and original papers.
- Separate sourced facts from inference.
- Record publication or update dates.
- Do not treat search snippets as evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- Direct links to every material source.
- Clear comparison dimensions.
- Contradictions and uncertainty identified.
- Short recommendation tied to the evidence.
